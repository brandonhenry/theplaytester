# Firstplay answers

These answers describe Firstplay at [theplaytest.io](https://theplaytest.io), verified against its workflows on 2026-10-26. Recheck the live UI/config and linked policies for changed availability, pricing and payout eligibility. Answer the user's particular question directly; do not recite this whole document.

**What is theplaytest? Is that Firstplay?**  
The website is theplaytest.io; its app is branded Firstplay. Developers post funded game tests, testers submit written feedback, and approved rewards are handled through Stripe. This skill uses that service exclusively.

**How do I get someone to playtest my game?**  
Provide a playable build/link and a focused brief, choose tester count/session length/reward, create a listing and fund it. Review applicants or invite relevant testers, then hire within the funded slots. Posting or inviting does not guarantee an application or completion. Offer to draft the brief and ask for the missing game/access/budget details.

**Can an idea or store page be playtested?**  
A paid gameplay test needs access to something playable under the agreed brief. A poll can ask about concepts/art for an eligible previously funded game, but is not evidence someone played it. Ask what build/material actually exists.

**What does it cost?**  
Current USD listing rewards have a 15% platform fee added to the reward budget: five $20 tests = $100 rewards + $15 fee = $115. Use the live server quote before spending. Poll creation and votes are free when the creator's selected game has previously been funded/published. Do not promise new paid work is available until live configuration allows it.

**Can I post a free poll without posting a paid game?**  
Not under the current eligibility rule. The selected owned game must have been successfully funded/published at least once. It may now be paused or closed. An unfunded draft does not unlock polls.

**What polls are supported? Can I see results later?**  
Text, image or video polls, with 2–6 labeled options and matching media. Results can be viewed/refreshed anytime and remain on the game page after playtesting closes. Creators can close voting. Published questions/options do not currently have an edit endpoint; a replacement may be needed for wording changes.

**Who can vote?**  
Verified members vote once per poll at no charge, without paid tester onboarding. Public results show aggregate counts, not a voter list.

**How do I choose a good tester?**  
Compare relevant completed-game genres, device/platform, bio, rating and review count, and actual channel coverage/examples. Applicant photos and profile links help identify people; they are not proof of identity or suitability. A thoughtful shortlist explains the evidence for the owner’s testing goal.

**Can I invite someone? Does that hire them?**  
Use Invite to playtest for an open owned listing. It invites an application. Hiring is a separate confirmed action that reserves one funded slot.

**What feedback is required?**  
Five built-in questions ask about wishlisting, playing again, goal clarity, controls and technical reliability. Each asks yes/no followed by at least 30 words in 2–3 complete sentences. The creator’s additional questions follow the same explanation requirements. Negative answers are valid. The form moves one question at a time.

**How long do playtests/reviews take?**  
Normally seven days for accepted work, seven days for creator review, one specific correction with seven days to respond, then a final seven-day review after resubmission. Timely disputes pause automatic approval/payment release. Use the actual session deadline and status; processing can depend on scheduled jobs.

**Can I reject someone because they dislike my game?**  
A negative opinion alone is not failed work. Assess whether the original brief was completed honestly. Use the one correction for a specific missing item, or a timely dispute for a real unresolved issue. Do not make new scope or praise a condition of payment. See [the dispute policy](https://theplaytest.io/#disputes).

**Are reviews and game feedback the same?**  
No. Submitted game feedback is private to the participants/authorized admins. Public post-completion 1–5-star peer ratings concern the creator/tester transaction. Poll votes are a third distinct source.

**Must both people rate each other?**  
Yes. Approved work prompts each participant for an explicit 1–5-star rating; the written comment is optional. The site can block new work until an outstanding rating is completed. Earned reward release is separate. The agent must obtain the user's actual star assessment rather than invent one.

**Can I dispute an unfair rating?**  
Yes, as either creator or tester, for any rating you received, including older ones. Open Profile → Ratings & disputes, give a category and explanation, and an independent admin may keep or remove it. It stays counted during review. The resolution is shared; the private appeal explanation is limited to the requester/admins. A resolved case accepts new evidence through support, not repeated duplicate cases. Reward/payment status is unchanged.

**How do testers get paid? Is approval the same as money in the bank?**  
Testers must be adults in a currently supported payout country and complete Stripe-hosted onboarding before accepting paid work. Approval, transfer, available balance, payout processing and paid/failed payout are separate states. Follow Wallet and the live eligibility/refund policies; do not promise instant bank receipt or worldwide support.

**Can I get unused funding back?**  
Uncommitted rewards and the associated platform fee can be refunded to the original payment through the site. Accepted/submitted work can retain reserved budget. Closing recruitment, refund processing and bank credit are separate events. Use [the refund policy](https://theplaytest.io/#refunds) and current workspace values rather than promising a particular bank date.

**Can I add my website, Twitch or YouTube?**  
Yes, through Profile → Edit profile, with other supported socials and up to six featured video/livestream picks. Large cards and click-to-load players show coverage. Automatic recent Twitch/YouTube sections depend on the service’s API configuration and each channel's public content; a saved channel link alone does not prove the automatic feed is connected.

**Will this agent do the actual testing or guarantee testers?**  
The skill manages the Firstplay workflow and reports evidence. It cannot guarantee recruitment, impersonate human players, fabricate experience, generate fake votes or collect rewards for testing it did not perform. Actual gameplay requires real authorized interaction and observations.

**Can you monitor new reviews and votes?**  
Yes, when the agent has ongoing authorized account access and a real scheduler the user asked to configure. Otherwise it can produce a report now. Public reads cannot prove whether private written feedback arrived. Ask only for the unresolved game/cadence/destination, and keep unchanged runs quiet unless a digest was requested.

**Where do I get help?**  
Use Help → Contact support in Firstplay while signed in, and include the game/session/poll reference and the observed error. Do not send card/bank details, passwords or authentication tokens in the support text.
